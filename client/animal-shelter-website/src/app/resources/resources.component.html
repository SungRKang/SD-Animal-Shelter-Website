Resources for New Dog Owners

Being a new dog owner can be an exciting but challenging experience. Here are some resources that can help you navigate this new journey:

  1. Books:

    • "The Art of Raising a Puppy" by The Monks of New Skete
    • "How to Be Your Dog's Best Friend: The Classic Training Manual for Dog Owners" by The Monks of New Skete
    • "The Other End of the Leash: Why We Do What We Do Around Dogs" by Patricia McConnell
    • "Perfect Puppy in 7 Days: How to Start Your Puppy Off Right" by Dr. Sophia Yin

  2. Websites:

    • American Kennel Club (AKC)
    • The Humane Society
    • ASPCA
    • Petfinder

  3. Online Courses:

    • Udemy
    • Coursera
    • Dog Training by Kikopup

  4. Local Dog Trainers and Classes:

    Look for local dog training classes or trainers in your area. Many offer puppy kindergarten classes or basic obedience classes for new dog owners.

  5. Social Media:

    Follow reputable dog trainers and behaviorists on social media platforms like Instagram or Facebook for daily tips and advice.

  6. Veterinarian:

    Your vet is an invaluable resource for all things related to your dog's health, behavior, and well-being. Don't hesitate to ask questions during vet visits.

  7. Dog Owner Community Forums:

    Websites like Reddit's r/dogs or The Dog Forum offer communities where you can ask questions, seek advice, and share experiences with other dog owners.

  8. Local Pet Stores:

    Many pet stores offer free seminars or workshops on dog training, nutrition, and health care. Check with your local pet store for upcoming events.
